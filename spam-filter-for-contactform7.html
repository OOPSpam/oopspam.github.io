The problem

Why Contact Form 7 still gets spam

Contact Form 7 runs on millions of WordPress sites, so spam bots look for it first. Many forms are left without any protection after setup.

Its built-in tools stop simple bots, but none of them read the message itself. Spam typed by real people, and bots that behave like people, still get through.

Contact Form 7 also doesn't save submissions by default, so it's hard to see what was blocked and why.

reCAPTCHA v3 Scores visitors in the background, but loads a Google script on every page and can't judge the message itself.
Cloudflare Turnstile Good at stopping bots without puzzles. Doesn't stop spam typed by people.
Quiz field Asks visitors a question. Extra friction for real people, and easy for a human spammer to answer.
Akismet Built for blog comments rather than forms, and needs a paid plan on commercial sites.

How it works

Set up in about five minutes

No changes to your forms, and nothing for your visitors to solve.

  1. Install the plugin

    In WordPress, go to Plugins → Add New, search for “oopspam Anti-Spam”, then install and activate it.

  2. Add your API key

    Create a free account, copy your API key and paste it under Settings → oopspam Anti-Spam.

  3. Turn on Contact Form 7

    In the Contact Form 7 section of the settings, switch on Activate Spam Protection and write the message spammers will see. That's it.

What happens to a blocked submission? The visitor sees the message you wrote, and the submission is not sent to your inbox. It is saved in the plugin's Spam Entries log with its score and the reason it was blocked. If you use Flamingo, the oopspam score shows there too.

FAQ

Contact Form 7 spam: common questions

Does oopspam work with Contact Form 7's reCAPTCHA or Turnstile?

Yes. oopspam runs alongside them, so you can keep them or remove them. Without reCAPTCHA, your pages no longer have to load Google's script.

Do I need Flamingo?

No. The plugin keeps its own Spam Entries log in your WordPress database. Flamingo is optional, and if you use it, the oopspam score appears there as well.

My form has more than one message field. Does that matter?

By default, oopspam checks your form's message field. If a form has several text areas, or you use different field names across forms, enter the message field names under Content field mapping, separated by commas.

Do I need to change my forms?

No. Switch on protection once in the plugin settings and it covers every Contact Form 7 form on your site.

Will it slow down my site?

No. The check happens on your server when the form is submitted. Nothing extra loads in your visitors' browsers.

What if a real message gets blocked?

Find it in the plugin's Spam Entries log, which shows why it was blocked. To make sure it doesn't happen again, add the sender's email or IP address to your allowed list, or lower the sensitivity level.

Is oopspam GDPR compliant?

Yes. Logs stay in your own WordPress database, and nothing is stored on our servers unless you turn on remote logging. You can also switch off checks that use personal data. See our GDPR notice and Data Processing Agreement.

How much does it cost?

Every account includes 40 free spam checks a month. Paid plans are priced by the number of checks, not the number of sites, so one API key covers every website you manage. See the pricing above.
