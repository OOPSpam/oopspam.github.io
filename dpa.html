Data Processing Agreement - OOPSpam LLC

This document lays out the responsibilities of OOPSpam LLC, hereafter referred to as OOPSpam, to its customers with regards to data protection in general and the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) specifically.

In consideration of the mutual obligations set out herein, the parties agree that this DPA shall be added to and form part of the Terms, and that except where context requires otherwise, references in this DPA to the Terms are to the Terms as amended by and including this DPA.

1. OOPSpam as Data Processor, Definitions

  • OOPSpam is a Data Processor operating on behalf of its customers.
  • Customers are individuals or organizations paying money to use the OOPSpam service. Free trial users of the OOPSpam Service are not Customers and should not send OOPSpam personal data.
  • OOPSpam's Customers are Data Controllers.
  • “Personal data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
  • “Data Protection Laws” means EU Directive 95/46/EC, as transposed into domestic legislation of each Member State and as amended, replaced or superseded from time to time, including by the GDPR and laws implementing or supplementing the GDPR.
  • "Services" means the OOPSpam's APIs (application program interface) and the professional services provided by OOPSpam.
  • “Sub-processor” means any Data Processor engaged by OOPSpam.
  • “Data Subject” means the individual to whom Personal Data relates.

2. Processing of Personal Data

  • Use of the service implies that OOPSpam may process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller in accordance with the requirements of Data Protection Laws. The Data Controller will ensure that instructions to OOPSpam for the processing of personal data comply with Data Protection Laws. The Data Controller is solely responsibility for the accuracy, quality, and legality of Personal Data and the means by which it acquires personal data.
  • In connection with the provision of the OOPSpam Service, the Data Controller transmits, and OOPSpam processes on the Data Controller’s behalf, IP addresses, email addresses, and the content of form submissions, messages or comments (free text), for the purpose of spam and fraud detection. The Data Controller may optionally pass additional parameters via the API (for example, a name or phone number), but these are not required and are not transferred by default. Request bodies are not stored by default; they are processed in memory only. The Data Controller bears sole responsibility for the lawfulness of the personal data it transmits.
  • Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the transfer is subject to the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two – Controller to Processor) and, where applicable, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, as completed and executed between OOPSpam and the Data Controller. A copy of the completed clauses is available on request from contact@oopspam.com.
  • OOPSpam lays out a full and accurate description of its data protection practices on its website at https://www.oopspam.com/gdpr. This description is updated from time to time as and when practices change.

3. Rights of Data Subjects

  • The Data Controller is solely responsible for the collecting of all necessary consent from Data Subjects to allow OOPSpam to process personal data on its behalf.
  • OOPSpam will, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify the Data Controller if it receives a request from a Data Subject for access to, or deletion of, that person’s personal data. OOPSpam will not respond to a Data Subject request without the Data Controllers prior written consent except to confirm that the request relates to the Data Controller. The Data Controller is solely responsible for completing such request as required by law.

4. Personnel

  • OOPSpam ensures that its personnel engaged in the processing of personal data are informed of the confidential nature of the personal data, have received appropriate training on their responsibilities and have agreed to confidentiality obligations that survive the termination of that persons’ employment or engagement by OOPSpam.
  • OOPSpam shall take commercially reasonable steps to ensure the reliability of any OOPSpam personnel engaged in the processing of personal data and that access to personal data by OOPSpam is limited to those OOPSpam personnel who require such access to perform the Services.
  • OOPSpam’s data protection officer can be reached by email at contact@oopspam.com

5. Sub-Processors

  • The Data Controller grants OOPSpam general written authorisation to engage the Sub-processors listed in the Annex to this DPA for the purposes of providing the Services. A Sub-processor may access personal data, and may appoint further sub-processors, only for the purposes of providing the services OOPSpam retained it to provide and not for any other purpose.
  • OOPSpam will notify the Data Controller in writing of any intended addition or replacement of Sub-processors at least 30 days in advance, and will provide the Data Controller with sufficient information to enable it to exercise its right to object. If the Data Controller objects to a proposed Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds, OOPSpam will either not engage the Sub-processor or, where OOPSpam is unable to do so, the Data Controller may terminate the affected processing without penalty.
  • OOPSpam will enter into a written contract with each Sub-processor that imposes data protection obligations at least as protective as those set out in this DPA, and will remain fully responsible to the Data Controller for the performance of each Sub-processor’s obligations.

6. Security

  • OOPSpam agrees to implement and maintain the administrative, technical, and physical safeguards of personal data stored using the Services.

7. Security Breach Management and Notification

  • If OOPSpam becomes aware of unlawful access to the Data Controller's personal data stored through the Services, or unauthorized access to the Services resulting in loss, disclosure, or alteration of the Data Controller's personal data (“Security Breach”), OOPSpam will promptly: (a) notify the Data Controller of the Security Breach; (b) investigate the Security Breach and provide the Data Controller with information known to OOPSpam about the Security Breach; and (c) follow its policies and procedures to mitigate the effects and to minimize any damage resulting from the Security Breach.
  • The Data Controller agrees that an unsuccessful Security Breach attempt will not be subject to Section 7.1 above. An unsuccessful Security Breach attempt is one that results in no unauthorized access to the Data Controller's personal data or to the Services storing your Personal Data, and may include, without limitation, pings and other broadcast attacks on firewalls or edge servers, port scans, unsuccessful log-on attempts, denial of service attacks, packet sniffing (or other unauthorized access to traffic data that does not result in access beyond IP addresses or headers) or similar incidents.
  • Notification(s) of Security Breaches, if any, will be delivered to one or more of the Customer’s business, technical or administrative contacts by any means OOPSpam selects, including via email. It is Customer’s sole responsibility to ensure it maintains accurate contact information on OOPSpam’s support systems at all times.
  • OOPSpam’s report of and/or response to a Security Breach under this Section will not be construed as an admission by OOPSpam to fault or liability with respect to the Security Breach.

8. Deletion and Return of Customer Data; Audit

  • Upon termination or expiry of the Services, OOPSpam will, at the Data Controller’s choice, either delete all personal data processed on behalf of the Data Controller or return it to the Data Controller, and will delete existing copies unless retention is required by Data Protection Laws or applicable tax or accounting obligations. At the Data Controller’s request, OOPSpam will certify that such deletion or return has been completed.
  • OOPSpam will make available to the Data Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with its obligations under this DPA, and, at the Data Controller’s request and with reasonable notice, will allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, of its processing activities conducted by the Data Controller or by an independent auditor mandated by it. Audits will be carried out during normal business hours, at the Data Controller’s expense, and in a manner that does not disrupt OOPSpam’s operations. Where OOPSpam holds a recognised certification or independent audit report covering the processing, OOPSpam may make such report available to the Data Controller in lieu of a direct audit.

9. Governing Laws

  • This Agreement is governed by the laws of Minnesota in the United States

10. Legal Effect

  • This agreement comes into effect from the time of purchase of an OOPSpam subscription. It expires with cessation of the Customer's OOPSpam subscription.
