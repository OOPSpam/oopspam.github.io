Infrastructure and hosting

The API runs in six regions worldwide and fails over automatically if a region becomes unavailable. EU requests are normally served in Frankfurt; if that region is unavailable, another region, including the United States, may be used. All production systems run with established cloud providers, whose physical security is covered by their own certifications. See our sub-processors.

Access control

  • Production access is limited to named team members who need it, on a least-privilege basis.
  • Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every administrative account, and the administrative area sits behind an additional access layer, so administrators authenticate twice.
  • Access is removed on the same day someone leaves and reviewed at least once a year.
  • Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality and completes security, privacy and phishing training every year.

Your account: passwords of at least 12 characters, optional two-factor authentication, and lockout after repeated failed sign-ins. Each account has its own API key, which you can rotate; IP restrictions for API keys are available on higher plans.

Application security and testing

  • Every code change goes through automated checks before deployment.
  • Dependencies are monitored continuously for known vulnerabilities.
  • Security patches are applied within 24 hours of our becoming aware of a vulnerability; critical dependency vulnerabilities the same day.
  • We penetration-test the API and dashboard every year, following the OWASP Testing Guide. Most recent: September 2026.

Incident response and continuity

We follow a documented incident response process. If a personal data breach affects your data, we notify you without undue delay, as set out in our DPA.

Databases are backed up daily and backups are kept for 7 days. We test disaster recovery every year, including regional failover and restoring from backup. Our recovery time objective is 24 hours or less.

You can check your logging status and delete stored data at any time from the Trust Center in your dashboard. At the end of the service, we delete your data. Details are in our DPA and on our GDPR page.

Compliance

  • CSA STAR Level 1: our CAIQ self-assessment is public in the CSA STAR Registry.
  • GDPR: our Data Processing Agreement applies automatically to every account. For transfers outside the EEA it includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two) and the UK Addendum.
  • PCI DSS: not applicable. We never receive card data.
