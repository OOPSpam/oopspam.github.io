Clean ActiveCampaign lists without exports

The ActiveCampaign integration lives inside your oopspam dashboard. Connect once, pull your contacts through the API, and review the list inside oopspam instead of working through manual exports and imports.

Typical workflow

1. Connect your ActiveCampaign account.

2. Pull one or more email lists into oopspam.

3. Review spammy, risky and suspicious contacts.

4. Unsubscribe flagged emails or delete the contact completely.

Review suspicious contacts before they affect your next send

Low quality email campaigns result in high bounce rates, create noise in automations, and make your outreach harder to trust.

oopspam helps your team surface suspicious contacts earlier so you can clean the list before your next broadcast or workflow runs.

Direct dashboard workflow, privacy-friendly by design

No contact information is saved on our servers. oopspam processes the data in real-time, surfaces the results for your review, and then discards it. We never store or log any contact information.
You stay in control of every cleanup action. Review the results first, then decide whether a contact should be unsubscribed or deleted.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the ActiveCampaign integration work?

Connect your ActiveCampaign account to oopspam, pull your lists into the dashboard, scan contacts for spammy, risky and suspicious signals, then review the flagged results.

Do I need to export contacts from ActiveCampaign?

No. The workflow is designed around a direct API connection, so you can review contacts inside oopspam without managing CSV exports.

What can I do with flagged contacts?

After a scan, you can review flagged contacts and choose whether to unsubscribe them from the list or delete the contact completely.
